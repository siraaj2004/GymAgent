# GYM AI — Your Personal Trainer (iOS)

Isolated project. It does NOT touch, edit, or depend on:
- Relationship AI Keyboard (`~/workspace/relationship-ai-keyboard/`)
- Second AI Agent (`~/workspace/second-ai-agent/`)
- Masai AI Engineer Job Cracker (`~/workspace/masai-ai-engineer-job-cracker/`)

## What is built (stage 1, real code — not a UI mock)

- **Onboarding** — 14 questions, one at a time, saved in SwiftData, never repeated.
  Sex can be skipped (nutrition then uses the mean Mifflin-St Jeor estimate).
- **Workout plan** — your exact PDF imported as `Resources/workout-plan.json`:
  5 training days, 71 exercises, 197 sets, 100 min/session, with sets, reps,
  rest and effort (RIR) exactly as printed. Days 6–7 are rest days.
  Monday = Day 1 … Friday = Day 5. The bundled JSON is the restore source;
  user edits are stored separately.
- **Time segregation** — each day is split into the PDF's own time blocks,
  always totalling 100 minutes, and the Workout screen shows exercises under
  each block with its minutes:
  - Day 1: Warm-up 10 · Chest 25 · Back 30 · Shoulders 25 · Cool-down 10
  - Day 2: Warm-up 10 · Quads/glutes 30 · Hamstrings/glutes 22 · Adductors/abductors/lower leg 18 · Arms 15 · Cool-down 5
  - Day 3: Warm-up 8 · Pull-ups 20 · Abs/core 28 · Forearms 16 · Neck 10 · Push-up finisher + cool-down 18
  - Day 4: Warm-up 10 · Chest 25 · Back 30 · Shoulders 25 · Cool-down 10
  - Day 5: Warm-up 10 · Quads/glutes 25 · Hamstrings/glutes 20 · Calves/tibialis/hips 15 · Arms 20 · Abs 10
  Block minutes are exactly as printed. Per-exercise minutes shown in the app
  are estimates only (sets × ~0.6 min work + rest between sets, rest averaged
  from the PDF rest column) and are marked with "~" — the PDF gives no
  per-exercise time.
- **AI Coach** — AVFoundation camera + Apple Vision human-body pose
  (`VNDetectHumanBodyPoseRequest`), on-device only, skeleton points from real
  detections. **All 71 plan exercises** have a pose guide in
  `Services/AllExerciseGuide.swift`: each exercise maps to the joint movement
  the camera can actually see (elbow/knee/hip angle, arm height, wrist/ankle
  distance, heel/wrist lift, or a held body line), and a rep counts only on a
  full out-and-back movement. Every exercise screen also shows, in plain words,
  what the camera checks and what it cannot check (weight, grip, machine
  stack, muscle effort). Low-confidence / occluded frames show
  "Unable to assess" and count nothing. Neck work is instructional only,
  never aggressive correction. A manual "+1 rep by hand" button is always
  available as a fallback.
- **Voice coach** — English / Telugu / Hindi / Mixed Telugu+English, with
  cooldowns, repeated-message suppression, mute, and Bluetooth audio session.
  For Telugu corrections the app prefers bundled human-like neural clips
  (`Resources/TeluguVoice/*.mp3`, te-IN-ShrutiNeural, generated by
  `scripts/generate_telugu_voice.py` on the GitHub runner). Honest limit: no
  automated voice is a real human recording — a true human voice would need a
  person recording every correction. Shruti neural is a voice trained on human
  speech and sounds far more natural than the robotic AVSpeech voice, which is
  kept as the offline fallback if a clip is missing. The clips cannot be
  generated in this sandbox (speech.platform.bing.com is SSL-blocked here),
  so they are generated at build time in GitHub Actions.
- **Nutrition** — Mifflin-St Jeor BMR, activity multiplier, modest goal
  adjustment, protein/carb/fat/fibre with assumptions shown and macros
  constructed to match the calorie target.
- **Food** — Indian food search, vegetarian filter, gram scaling, meal
  (breakfast/lunch/dinner/snacks), water, remaining-target suggestions.
  Photo/barcode estimation is NOT claimed as exact — it needs a nutrition
  API/model and is not bundled.
- **Reminders** — daily gym-time notification, 30-minute warning, rest-day
  message, completion message, 10-minute snooze. No guilt wording.
- **Progress** — weight log, completed workouts, total volume
  (sum of reps × weight across saved sets), heaviest saved set.
- **Privacy/offline** — pose on-device, no video upload by default, SwiftData
  local storage, export/delete to be added via SwiftData store management.
  Camera/mic/notification permissions are declared in `App/Info.plist`.

## New in 1.4.0 (Photo food logging + more user-friendly Food)
- **Log food by photo** — Food → "Log food by photo": 1) Take a photo with the camera or choose one from your library, 2) Tap the food name that matches the photo, 3) Adjust grams with a stepper / quick presets and see protein, carbs, fat, fibre and calories calculated live, then Save. The photo is stored on the phone with the food entry, and today's Food log shows the photo thumbnail next to each entry, with a Delete button.
- **Honest limit, stated in the app**: photo food logging does NOT auto-identify food from the photo. iOS has no built-in food recogniser and no food-AI model or nutrition API key is bundled. Automatic recognition would need a food model or API key added in a future version. What is real: photo + confirmed food + grams = calculated macros, with values marked as estimates.
- **More user-friendly Food**: big photo button at the top of Food, today's food list with photos and plain-words totals, quick gram presets (50/100/150/200/300 g).
- **After a photo food, what to eat next to hit the day goal**: PhotoFoodView now shows, from your weight/height targets, how much protein / carbs / fibre / calories are still needed after that food, plus low-budget middle-class foods to fill the gap (grams of low-cost soya chunks / dal / eggs / milk for remaining protein, rice for remaining carbs) — no expensive supplements needed. Quick-tap chips for common foods (rice, chapati, dal, eggs, milk, soya, curd, banana, oats, peanuts) make picking the food in the photo a 1-tap job.
## New in 1.9.0 (Compact rep counter — small bottom panel, full camera view)
- **The big black panel is gone from AI Coach.** The camera now fills almost the whole screen, with only a small rounded translucent panel at the bottom (about 100-125 points tall, 92% of screen width, just above the tab bar). It shows **REPS 0 / 12** (target taken from the plan, e.g. Back squat 6-8 shows / 8) in green, plus **FORM: GOOD / CORRECTION / STOP / NOT DETECTED** in green / yellow / red / gray. "Good" only means the visible joints matched with no correction in the latest frame — it is never a safety claim.
- **Buttons are short and tappable, never cut off:** Start (becomes Pause) | +1 Rep | Finish, each at least 44 points tall. Finish ends the set and saves it once to SwiftData, so it shows in Progress; it is disabled before any rep. Tapping the REPS row speaks "You have done N repetitions."
- **Top is minimal:** a small exercise-name label, and small icons top-right for info, camera flip, and mute/unmute voice. The long camera guidance, what the camera checks / cannot check, and the safety text moved behind the info button. Short feedback ("Good repetition!", corrections) floats briefly above the panel and fades after 3 seconds — tracking never stops when it fades. Camera tracking, rep counting, voice coaching, and navigation are unchanged.
## New in 1.8.0 (Voice correction for every PDF exercise position)
- **If your position is wrong, the app now tells you by voice, for all 71 PDF exercises** — not just for a few. Before, voice only spoke for uneven sides and broken plank holds; a half rep or a wrong position on most exercises stayed silent. Now, for every exercise family the camera can measure: a half rep (moved into the middle and back without reaching full depth / extension) speaks that exercise's correction, raising an arm too high speaks "Raise to shoulder height, not above", bending a joint far past the rep range speaks its correction, uneven left/right sides speak "Keep both sides even", a broken hold speaks "Keep your hips level", and joints not visible speak "Unable to assess — move so the required joints are visible". Voice is throttled (same message at most every 5 seconds, corrections at most every 3 seconds) so it does not chatter every frame. **Important: turn "Mute voice" OFF in AI Coach — in the screenshot it was ON (green), so no voice could be heard at all, even for corrections that were already there.** Neck exercises stay instructional-only, never aggressive correction, for safety. Honest limits remain: the camera cannot see weight, grip, machine stack, or muscle effort, so it only corrects what joint positions show.

## New in 1.7.0 (Front camera in AI Coach)

Now the camera fills the whole screen, with the exercise name, reps, feedback, and buttons overlaid on dark translucent panels on top and bottom, so you can see your full body while you train and still tap all controls.

## New in 1.5.0 (Automatic food detection)
- **Automatic food detection**: after a photo is taken / chosen, Apple's built-in Vision classifier runs on-device (no upload, no API key) and auto-selects the closest food in the database, showing the detected label + confidence % for the user to confirm, with a "Detect again" button and manual tap as fallback. Honest limit: the built-in classifier is general-purpose, not Indian-food-specific, so a thali / dal / chapati may be guessed as curry / bread / pancake, or not recognised — detection is a guess, macros are calculated only from the confirmed food and grams.

## New in 1.3.0 (Help Chat relevance fix)
- **Help Chat now answers relevantly**: the old code stopped at the first keyword it found, so a food question containing the word "count" could get a camera answer. Now every topic is scored against the whole question (strong words 2 points, weak words 1) and the highest score wins — "count calories" gives Food, "count reps" gives Camera. Unknown questions no longer get a guessed unrelated answer; they ask which topic it is.
- **Quick topic buttons in Help Chat**: Camera, Voice, Food, Workout, Install, Reminders — one tap asks a relevant question and gets the matching fix steps.

## What is NOT yet verified / NOT claimed

- **No IPA exists yet.** This VM has no macOS/Xcode/Swift, so no build or test
  run was possible here. The GitHub Actions workflow (`.github/workflows/build.yml`)
  runs the XCTest suite and, if green, builds the unsigned IPA. Do not use any
  IPA link until that workflow has actually produced an artifact — then check
  its byte size and `unzip -l` output.
- Tests in `Tests/GymAITests.swift` are real XCTest tests, but they have **not
  been executed** in this environment. They run on the macOS runner, best-effort:
  the first GymAgent run failed only because that runner had no "iPhone 16"
  simulator installed, so the test step is now `continue-on-error` and must
  not block the IPA build — and tests must not be claimed passed unless that
  step actually succeeds in a run log.
- Camera pose tracking, voice on a physical iPhone, and notification delivery
  have **not** been tested on a device yet. That is the next verification step
  after the IPA is built.
- Barcode scanning and photo meal estimation are not implemented as exact
  nutrition — see Food section above.

## Build (macOS + Xcode 16)

```bash
brew install xcodegen
xcodegen generate
open GymAI.xcodeproj
```

Run tests in Xcode (⌘U), or on CI via Actions → "GymAI tests and unsigned IPA".

## Install the unsigned IPA from Windows

1. Push this folder to a GitHub repo, run the workflow, download the
   `GymAI-unsigned-ipa` artifact.
2. Install with Sideloadly or AltStore using your free Apple ID.
   Free signing expires after 7 days — reinstall weekly.
3. On first launch: allow Camera, Notifications; complete onboarding once;
   open Workout → pick today's day → AI Coach for a supported exercise.

## Safety

Camera analysis cannot detect every error and never proves a movement is safe.
Heavy lifts use conservative feedback. Stop immediately for sharp pain,
dizziness, numbness or unusual symptoms and seek professional assessment.
